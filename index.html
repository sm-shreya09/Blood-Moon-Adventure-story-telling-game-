🌙 BLOOD MOON ADVENTURE 🌙

🧙‍♂ Fantasy Quest

You are a young mage on the eve of your initiation, in a kingdom where the sky glows red under a looming blood moon. Ancient prophecies whisper that you alone may stop the rise of a dark sorcerer.

🚀 Sci-Fi Survival

Your starship has crashed on a hostile alien world. The air crackles with static, strange constellations burn overhead, and something huge moves just beyond the wreckage light.

🕵‍♂ Mystery Detective

On a stormy night, the city museum reports the impossible: its most guarded artifact, the Jade Dragon, has vanished from a sealed vault. Everyone has secrets. Only you have the questions.

👻 Horror Night

On an empty highway under the blood moon, your car dies in front of a forgotten mansion. The lights inside flicker, the woods whisper your name, and the night feels very, very wrong.
